About me

Hi, I’m Anouk Fontaine, a UX & data designer from Switzerland, based in Hanoi, Vietnam. Driven by a desire to make things simpler and more efficient in an increasingly complex world, my journey has taken me from business studies through hospitality, HR and development finance to UX design. Turns out, making complicated things simpler is what I love doing most.

I speak French, (Swiss) German and English, sometimes all in the same sentence. Outside of work, you’ll find me writing songs, taking photos or hunting for the perfect bowl of noodle soup.

Over 9 years of professional experience in various fields
